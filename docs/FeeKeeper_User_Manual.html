Client Operating Manual & System Guide

Welcome to FeeKeeper, the complete fee management platform engineered for gym owners, fitness clubs, coaching academies, and martial arts dojos. This guide demonstrates how to register, manage members, record instant payments, send branded WhatsApp receipts, and configure automated reminder notifications.

📱 Protected Self-Service Onboarding
To protect against fake accounts and abuse, FeeKeeper requires an authorized Registration Secret Key (zovak2026) during onboarding. Clients without a key can contact the administrator directly on WhatsApp (+91 96063 95170) to activate their business.

1. Getting Started & Account Registration

To start using FeeKeeper, access the web portal at /register. Registration creates a fully isolated, secure tenant workspace for your academy.

  • Business Name: The official name of your gym, club, or tutorial center (e.g., Titan Fitness Academy).
  • Owner Name: Primary proprietor or branch manager full name.
  • Mobile Number: 10-digit primary mobile number for official communication.
  • UPI ID (Optional): Your business UPI VPA (e.g., titanfitness@okaxis) to embed on receipts.
  • Email & Password: Administrator login credentials.
  • Registration Secret Key: Official activation key provided by the administrator (e.g. zovak2026).

2. Dashboard Overview & Key Metrics

Upon logging in, the central dashboard provides instant, real-time metrics across your membership base:

  • Active Members: Members in good standing with valid, active training access.
  • Expiring in 5 Days: Members whose validity concludes within 5 calendar days.
  • Overdue Members: Members whose plan elapsed without a renewal payment.
  • Revenue This Month: Total collected membership fees across all payment modes.
Figure 2: Real-time operational ribbon and searchable members directory with color-coded status badges.

3. Member Management & Status Tracking

3.1 Adding a New Member

  1. Click + Add Member on the dashboard.
  2. Enter the member's full name and WhatsApp mobile number.
  3. Select or type the plan name (e.g., Gold Annual, Strength & Cardio, Monthly Coaching).
  4. Enter the plan fee amount and duration in days (e.g., 30, 90, 180, or 365).
  5. FeeKeeper automatically calculates the Expiry Date as Join Date + Plan Duration Days.

3.2 Understanding Member Status Badges

Status Badge Meaning & System Behavior Recommended Staff Action
ACTIVE Membership is current and valid. Grant normal desk check-in / entry.
EXPIRING SOON Plan expires within 5 days or fewer. Click WhatsApp to send friendly reminder.
OVERDUE Plan has expired without renewal. Flag at front desk; collect renewal via Mark paid.

4. Collecting Fees & Recording Payments ("Mark Paid")

FeeKeeper incorporates pessimistic database locking and idempotency protection. Even if front-desk staff accidentally double-clicks the payment confirmation button, duplicate receipts or duplicate fee charges are strictly blocked.

  1. Click the indigo Mark paid button beside any member.
  2. Verify the amount (or adjust for partial fee settlements).
  3. Optionally select an active Promotion Code to apply promotional discounts.
  4. Choose UPI (Instant Online/QR) or Cash (Front Desk Counter).
  5. Click Confirm payment. The member's status immediately updates to ACTIVE, expiry is extended, and the receipt dialog appears.

6. Receipt Distribution: PDF, Print Slip & WhatsApp

When a payment is completed, staff have 3 instant options at the bottom of the receipt modal:

  • 📥 Download PDF: Generates a publication-grade single-page A5 PDF certificate featuring your logo, contact numbers, itemized discount breakdown, and digital authorization seal.
  • 🖨️ Print Slip: Opens instant printing formatted for standard POS thermal receipt printers (80mm) or desktop office printers.
  • 💬 WhatsApp Receipt: Opens a pre-formatted WhatsApp chat directly with the member, delivering receipt number, plan details, payment mode, and renewed expiry date in one tap.

7. Automated WhatsApp Reminders & Notifications

FeeKeeper features an automated reminder engine designed to eliminate manual follow-ups:

  • Daily 10:00 AM Automated Trigger: Every morning at 10:00 AM in your configured regional time zone, FeeKeeper scans all member records. Any account marked Expiring Soon (within 5 days) or Overdue automatically receives an automated WhatsApp reminder template.
  • Pre-Configured WhatsApp Copy: Reminders include the member's name, plan name, days remaining, due amount, and your center's UPI payment link so members can renew online.
  • Manual 1-Click WhatsApp Button: Staff can click the WhatsApp button beside any member on the dashboard to trigger an on-demand reminder chat at any time.
Figure 5: Automated WhatsApp reminder card showing the single-row header with verified Sent status badge.

8. Seasonal Promotions & Discount Offers Engine

Run seasonal and holiday campaigns (e.g., New Year Resolution 20% OFF, Summer Fitness Camp ₹500 OFF):

  • Navigate to Dashboard > Offers to create promotional codes.
  • Supports both Percentage Discounts (e.g., 20%) and Flat Discounts (e.g., ₹500).
  • Set start and end dates; optionally restrict promo codes to specific membership durations (e.g. valid only for 365-day annual plans).
  • Discounts are automatically calculated and itemized on the member's digital and PDF receipts.

9. Business Settings & Multi-Currency Configuration

  • Global Currencies Supported: INR (₹), USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), AED (AED), CAD (C$), AUD (A$).
  • Time Zone Selection: Aligns daily 10:00 AM reminder triggers and receipt timestamps to your local time (e.g., Asia/Kolkata, America/New_York, Asia/Dubai, Europe/London).
  • Default Receipt Theme: Set your center's preferred default receipt style under Settings.
