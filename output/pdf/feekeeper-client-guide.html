SIMPLE FEE MANAGEMENT

Welcome to FeeKeeper

Practical guide for gyms, yoga studios, and tuition centres

Manage members, track membership expiry, record payments, and share reminders from one mobile-friendly dashboard.

QUICK START GUIDE

1. Create your business account

1. Open zovak.in/register.
2. Enter your business name, owner name, phone, UPI ID, email, and password.
3. Sign in and check your business details under Settings.
Keep your login private. Each business account has its own private member and payment records.

2. Add your members

From the dashboard, select Add Member and enter:

  • Member name and phone number
  • Plan name (for example, Monthly Gym or Yoga)
  • Plan amount
  • Join date
  • Plan duration: 30, 90, or 180 days

FeeKeeper calculates the expiry date from the join date and duration. A 30-day plan is a fixed 30 days; it does not automatically mean the same calendar day every month.

Import many members with CSV

Choose CSV template on the dashboard, fill the required columns, then select Import CSV. Required information: full name, phone, plan name, plan amount, duration, and join date. Dates use YYYY-MM-DD. Up to 500 rows can be uploaded in one file. Review the import result for row errors.

3. Understand member statuses

  • Active: membership has not reached its expiry date.
  • Expiring Soon: expiry is within the app's upcoming five-day window.
  • Overdue: the membership expiry date has passed and no renewal has been recorded.

Use the dashboard cards, search box, status filter, and page controls to find members. Search works with a name or phone number.

Missed fees: The current model tracks membership expiry and overdue status. It does not add a new monthly charge for each missed month. Record a payment when the member renews.

4. Record a payment and share a receipt

1. Find the member and tap Mark Paid.
2. Check or edit the amount, choose UPI or Cash, then confirm.
3. On success, the membership is renewed and its status updates.
4. Select Download receipt or Print receipt from the confirmation panel.

The receipt includes your business details, member, plan, payment amount and mode, receipt number, payment date, and new expiry date. Payment history is available from the payments view.

How renewal dates work

If the member renews before expiry, the added duration starts from the current expiry date. If already expired, it starts from the payment date. Example: a 90-day renewal adds 90 days using this rule.

5. Send a WhatsApp reminder

Use WhatsApp reminder on a member row to open WhatsApp with a message prepared for you. Review and send it in WhatsApp yourself. Automatic reminders are scheduled for members expiring in exactly three days and members one to seven days overdue, when a sending provider and approved templates are configured.

A prepared WhatsApp link does not send by itself. The current default provider may be mock until WhatsApp setup is completed.

6. Offers and settings

Promotions

In the dashboard's offers area, create a promotion with a code, discount, validity dates, and eligible plan duration if applicable. During payment, enter the code to preview its discount before confirming.

Business settings

Open Settings to update business name, owner name, phone, UPI ID, currency, and timezone where available. Select Logout when finished, especially on a shared device.

7. Helpful habits

  • Double-check the member and amount before confirming a payment.
  • Keep member phone numbers and business login credentials private.
  • Use CSV import results to correct any rejected rows.
  • Confirm your UPI ID and business details before sharing reminders or receipts.
  • Contact your FeeKeeper administrator/support contact if something looks incorrect.

FeeKeeper helps record memberships and payments. It does not collect payments from members or automatically charge them.
